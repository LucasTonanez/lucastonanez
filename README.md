# Hi, I'm Lucas Toñanez

### CS @ UCF '27 | Undergraduate Researcher @ UCF IAI | Multimodal AI • Computer Vision • Video Understanding

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Central Florida and an undergraduate researcher at the UCF Institute for Artificial Intelligence. My research interests center on **multimodal AI, long-form video understanding, temporal grounding, adaptive inference, and embodied AI**.

I'm a coauthor of **[Conditional Multi-Event Temporal Grounding in Long-Form Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15320)**. My contributions included work on **adaptive query planning, end-to-end multimodal temporal-grounding pipeline integration, benchmark/evaluation infrastructure, and video-understanding experiments**.

### Conditional Multi-Event Temporal Grounding in Long-Form Video — 2026
**Multimodal AI • Computer Vision • Long-Video Understanding**

Contributed across the research-method and research-engineering sides of long-form video temporal grounding, including:

- Query-aware planning that adapts downstream processing based on the natural-language query and initial video context
- Integration of independently developed segmentation, filtering, localization, verification, and memory components into an end-to-end pipeline
- Benchmark and evaluation infrastructure for structured temporal predictions, long-running experiments, and result validation

## Selected Projects

### [PCA + CRC Face Recognition](https://github.com/LucasTonanez/pca-crc-face-recognition)
Face recognition on LFW using PCA via SVD and a Collaborative Representation Classifier, with validation-based hyperparameter selection and held-out evaluation.

### [SVM Face Classification](https://github.com/LucasTonanez/svm-face-classification)
Linear and RBF SVM face classification on LFW with standardized preprocessing, validation tuning, optional PCA, and test-set evaluation.

### [MNIST — FCNN vs CNN](https://github.com/LucasTonanez/mnist-fcnn-vs-cnn)
Comparison of fully connected and convolutional neural networks for MNIST classification, including normalization, augmentation, and model-performance analysis.

### [Gasoline Price Prediction](https://github.com/LucasTonanez/gas-price-predictor)
Time-series forecasting project using FRED gasoline and WTI crude-oil data, comparing persistence, Linear, Ridge, and Random Forest models under chronological holdout evaluation.

### [MERN Video Game Store](https://github.com/LucasTonanez/MERN-Video-Game-Store)
Full-stack MERN marketplace with authentication, product browsing, protected routes, and MongoDB-backed account functionality.
